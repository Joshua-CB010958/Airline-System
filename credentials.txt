------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO LOG IN (Swagger UI)
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open http://localhost:8003/docs
2. Click POST /login -> Try it out
3. Enter username and password from above
4. Copy the access_token from the response
5. Click the Authorize button (top-right padlock icon)
6. Enter:  Bearer <paste_token_here>
7. Click Authorize -> Close
8. All protected endpoints will now use your token

Token expiry: 30 minutes (re-login to get a new token)
